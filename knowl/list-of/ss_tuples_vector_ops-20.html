Example 1.1.21. Linear combination: \(n\)-vectors.

Show that every vector in \(\R^3\) is a linear combination of the standard basis vectors \(\bolde_1\text{,}\) \(\bolde_2\text{,}\) and \(\bolde_3\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Given any vector \(\boldv=(a,b,c)\in \R^3\text{,}\) we have
\begin{align*} \boldv\amp =a\bolde_1+b\bolde_2+c\bolde_3 \text{.} \end{align*}
