For each defined sequence \(s=(a_i)_{i\in I}\text{,}\) expand \(s\) in the form \(s=(a_1,a_2,\dots)\) including at least the first four terms.
  1. \(\displaystyle s=(i)_{i=1}^\infty\)

    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle s=(2i+1)_{i=1}^\infty\)
    🔗
    🔗

    🔗
