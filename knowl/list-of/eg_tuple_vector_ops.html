Example 1.1.14. Tuple vector operations.

  1. Let \(\boldx=(1,2,1,1)\) and \(\boldy=(-3,-2,5,5)\text{.}\) Compute \((-2)\boldx+\boldy\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Consider the infinite sequences \(s=(1,0,1,0,\dots)\) and \(t=(0,1,0,1,\dots)\text{.}\) Compute \(s+t\) and \(2s+(-3)t\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Let \(A=\begin{amatrix} 1\amp 2\amp 3\\ 4\amp 5\amp 6 \end{amatrix}\) and \(B=\begin{amatrix} 1\amp -1\amp 0\\ 0\amp -1\amp -1 \end{amatrix}\text{.}\) Compute \(2A+(-7)B\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
  1. We have
    \begin{align*} (-2)\boldx+\boldy \amp = (-2,-4,-2,-2)+(-3,-2,5,5)\\ \amp = (-5,-6,3,3)\text{.} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. We have
    \begin{align*} s+t \amp = (1,0,1,0,\dots)+(0,1,0,1,\dots)\\ \amp = (1,1,1,1,\dots)\text{,} \end{align*}
    and
    \begin{align*} 2s+(-3)t \amp = (2,0,2,0,\dots)+(0,-3,0,-3,\dots)\\ \amp = (2,-3,2,-3,\dots)\text{.} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. We have
    \begin{align*} 2A+(-7)B \amp = 2\begin{amatrix} 1\amp 2\amp 3\\ 4\amp 5\amp 6 \end{amatrix}+(-7)\begin{amatrix} 1\amp -1\amp 0\\ 0\amp -1\amp -1 \end{amatrix}\\ \amp = \begin{amatrix} 2\amp 4\amp 6\\ 8\amp 10\amp 12 \end{amatrix}+\begin{amatrix} -7\amp 7\amp 0\\ 0\amp 7\amp 7 \end{amatrix}\\ \amp = \begin{amatrix} -5\amp 11\amp 6\\ 8\amp 17\amp 19 \end{amatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
