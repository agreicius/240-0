Example 1.1.9. Marketing tuples.

In our marketing example, the index set can be taken to be \(I=\{H, W, A, I\}\text{,}\) and a datum collected by our marketer is just an \(I\)-tuple \((a_H, a_W, a_A, a_I)\) that specifies the height \(a_H\text{,}\) weight \(a_W\text{,}\) age \(a_A\text{,}\) and income \(a_I\) of a given consumer.
