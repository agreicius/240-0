Proposition 1.1.10. Equality of \(n\)-tuples.

The \(m\)-tuple \((a_i)_{i=1}^m\) is equal to the \(n\)-tuple \((b_i)_{i=1}^n\) if and only if \(m=n\) and \(a_i=b_i\) for all \(i\in \{1,2,\dots, m\}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.

Unpacking the the notational conventions, we have \((a_i)_{i=1}^{m}=(a_i)_{i\in I}\text{,}\) where \(I=\{1,2,\dots, m\}\) and \((b_i)_{i=1}^{n}=(b_i)_{i\in J}\text{,}\) where \(J=\{1,2,\dots, n\}\text{.}\) By Definition 1.1.9, the two tuples are equal if and only if \(I=J\) and \(a_i=b_i\) for all \(i\in I\text{.}\) The first condition is equivalent to \(m=n\text{,}\) and the second condition is equivalent to \(a_i=b_i\) for all \(i\in \{1,2,\dots, m\}\text{.}\)
