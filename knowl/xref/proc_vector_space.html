Procedure 1.1.19. Verifying vector space axioms.

To introduce and verify a vector space, proceed as follows.
  1. Make explicit the underlying set \(V\) of the vector space.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Define the operations that serve as vector addition and scalar multiplication.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Identify the zero vector of \(V\) and verify that it satisfies the identity of Axiom iii.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Define the rule that assigns to each vector \(\boldv\in V\) its vector inverse \(-\boldv\) and verify that this definition of \(-\boldv\) satisfies the identity of Axiom iv.
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Verify that the vector operations satisfy Axioms i-ii and v-viii.
