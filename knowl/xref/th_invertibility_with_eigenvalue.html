Theorem 6.1.30. Invertibility theorem.

Let \(A\) be an \(n\times n\) matrix. The following statements are equivalent.
  1. \(A\) is invertible.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The matrix equation
    \begin{equation*} A\underset{n\times 1}{\boldx}=\underset{n\times 1}{\boldb} \end{equation*}
    has a unique solution for any \(\boldb\in \R^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The matrix equation
    \begin{equation*} A\underset{n\times 1}{\boldx}=\underset{n\times 1}{\boldb} \end{equation*}
    has a solution for any \(\boldb\in \R^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The matrix equation
    \begin{equation*} A\underset{n\times 1}{\boldx}=\underset{n\times 1}{\boldzero} \end{equation*}
    has a unique solution: namely, \(\boldx=\boldzero_{n\times 1}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(A\) is row equivalent to \(I_n\text{,}\) the \(n\times n\) identity matrix.
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \(A\) is a product of elementary matrices.
    🔗
    🔗
  7. \(\det A\ne 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  8. \(\displaystyle \NS A=\{\boldzero\}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  9. \(\displaystyle \nullity A=0\)
    🔗
    🔗
  10. \(\displaystyle \rank A=n\)
    🔗
    🔗
  11. \(\displaystyle \RS A=\R^n\)
    🔗
    🔗
  12. \(\displaystyle \CS A=\R^n\)
    🔗
    🔗
  13. Any of the following equivalent conditions about the set \(S\) of columns of \(A\) hold: \(S\) is a basis of \(\R^n\text{;}\) \(S\) spans \(\R^n\text{;}\) \(S\) is linearly independent.
    🔗
    🔗
  14. Any of the following equivalent conditions about the set \(S\) of rows of \(A\) hold: \(S\) is a basis of \(\R^n\text{;}\) \(S\) spans \(\R^n\text{;}\) \(S\) is linearly independent.
    🔗
    🔗
  15. The matrix transformationn \(T_A\colon \R^n\rightarrow \R^n\) is an isomorphism.
    🔗
    🔗
  16. \(0\) is not an eigenvalue of \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
